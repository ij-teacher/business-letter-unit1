## Letter parts

1. **Letterhead / Sender Information** — Who is sending the letter? Gives company name and contact information.
2. **Date** — When was the letter written?
3. **Reference Line (Ref)** — Which file, order, account, or project does the letter belong to?
4. **Inside Address** — Who is receiving the letter? Gives name, title, company, and address.
5. **Attention Line (Attn)** — Which person, team, or department should receive it?
6. **Salutation** — How is the recipient formally greeted?
7. **Subject Line (RE)** — What is the letter about?
8. **Opening Paragraph** — Why is the writer writing?
9. **Main Body** — What details, results, reasons, or evidence are needed?
10. **Closing / Call to Action** — What should the recipient do next?
11. **Complimentary Close** — How does the letter end politely?
12. **Signature and Typed Name, Job title** — Who takes responsibility for the message?
13. **Identification Initials** — Who wrote and who typed the letter?
14. **Enclosure Notation** — Which additional documents are included?
15. **Copy Notation (cc)** — Who else received a copy?

## Vocabulary

- **confirm**: officially verify that something is true or received (confirm receipt / confirm an order)
- **inspection**: a careful examination (final inspection / safety inspection)
- **thorough**: careful and complete (a thorough review)
- **meet**: satisfy a requirement (meet a deadline / meet standard)
- **capacity**: the maximum amount something can contain or support (load-bearing capacity / production capacity)
- **original**: the first version or initial plan (original document / original plan)
- **collaborate**: work together toward a goal (collaborate on a project)
- **proceed**: continue to the next stage (proceed with payment / proceed to the next phase)
- **determine**: decide or discover after consideration (determine the cause / determine that...)
- **stamp**: place an official mark or seal on a document (a stamped certificate)
